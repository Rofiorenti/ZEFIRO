Flight over water

Passenger boats have not changed in fifty years: loud, heavy, burning fuel to push water aside. Zefiro stops pushing. Above twelve knots it lifts clear of the surface and flies — no wake, no noise, no emissions.

The system

Two states,
one hull

The foil geometry comes straight out of the last America's Cup generation, reworked for passengers. A retraction mechanism — patent filed, UIBM n° 102025000015892 — folds the wings into the hull, so the same boat behaves like an ordinary tender at the dock and like an aircraft at speed.
